1The Vision

Each branch operates its own day-to-day system — registering members, logging walk-ins, and recording payments. Meanwhile, the head office sees everything in one place: consolidated sales, attendance, and membership across all branches, with the ability to verify and oversee each branch's records from a single dashboard. One source of truth for the whole company.

2What It Delivers

  • Per-branch operations — each branch runs independently with its own staff accounts
  • Head-office oversight — one consolidated dashboard across all branches
  • Role-based access — branch staff see only their branch; management sees everything
  • Consolidated reporting — company-wide sales & attendance, plus per-branch drill-down
  • Verification workflow — head office can review and confirm branch records
  • Full accountability — every action logged per user, per branch
